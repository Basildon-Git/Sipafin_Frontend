About us

A Zimbabwean business built around convenience

Shakenoak Investments combines mobile technology, financial agency services and car hire into one trusted, one-stop-shop business.

Who we are

Three divisions, one customer relationship

Shakenoak Investments is a dynamic and innovative Zimbabwean company operating primarily in the mobile phone and accessories retail sector, complemented by financial agency services and a newly established car-hire business unit. Customers can access technology products, financial transaction services and, increasingly, vehicle-hire services from one trusted business.

Our long-term objective is to become a recognised Zimbabwean brand for affordable technology, convenient financial services and reliable mobility solutions — first strengthening our existing Harare operations, then expanding into additional locations.

Mobile Phones & Accessories Our founding business — smartphones, feature phones and accessories at every budget.
Financial Agency Registered agent for CBZ, ZB, Stanbic Bank, POSB, FBC, O'mari, InnBucks and EcoCash.
Car Hire Our newest division, launched to diversify revenue and serve a broader customer base.
CEO's statement
"You cannot mandate productivity; you must provide the tools to let people become their best."

Shakenoak Investments has the potential to develop into a diversified Zimbabwean enterprise by combining three complementary business areas: mobile phones and accessories, financial agency services and car hire. Our greatest strategic advantage is the ability to bring several everyday services together in a single customer relationship.

Because our car-hire division is still new, we are prioritising operational controls, customer acquisition and fleet utilisation before making significant fleet investments — building the core business first, then scaling deliberately.

Brian S. Shoniwa Managing Director & CEO
